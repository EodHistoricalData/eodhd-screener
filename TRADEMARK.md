## The code licence does not grant trademark rights

The MIT licence covers copyright in the software. It grants no rights in any trademark — a name or a logo is not part of "the Software" it licenses. So the code is yours to use, change and sell under MIT; the marks are not, and permission for those comes only from this file.

Nothing here narrows the MIT licence. If the two ever seem to disagree about what you may do with the *code*, the licence wins.

## What you may do

- **Keep the marks where we shipped them.** The application displays EODHD branding by design. Running it, deploying it for permitted purposes, and leaving that branding in place is fine and needs no further permission.
- **Say what your work is built on**, factually and in plain words — "built with the EODHD API", "based on an EODHD sample application". Describing a fact about your software is not trademark use we object to.
- **Reproduce the marks in documentation, articles, tutorials and talks** when writing about this software or the EODHD API.

## What you may not do

- **Imply that we endorse, sponsor, or maintain your version.** If you change the software, make it clear the changes are yours. A modified copy must not present itself as an official EODHD release.
- **Use the marks in your own product name, company name, domain name, app-store listing, social media handle, or logo**, or in anything confusingly similar to them.
- **Alter the marks** — recolouring, restyling, redrawing or combining the logo with other elements.

## White-labelling

You do not need to ask. The MIT licence lets you modify the software, and that includes replacing our branding with your own — for a client-facing product, an internal tool, anything. Keep the copyright notice the licence asks for, put your own marks where ours were, and you are done.

The line this file draws is about *our* marks, not about whether you may remove them: do not ship something carrying EODHD branding that we did not publish.

If you see these marks used in a way that misrepresents EODHD, email **support@eodhistoricaldata.com**.
